HUMAN DIRECTION
Jack Byers set the requirements, supplied the Figma design, defined every widget,
and caught two significant defects: the misleading cross-format statistics and
the per-format sample-size limitation. Scope, pacing, and design decisions were
theirs throughout.

On 2026-08-09/10 they directed four further changes, each from their own reading
of the running app: the game count had to mean games *of the selected format*;
"Most Used Team" had to show whole teams rather than individual Pokemon; ranked
tables could choose a sort column but not flip its direction, because a 90% win
rate at the top of a "lost to" table misleads; and the placeholder nav links came
out. They also chose the hand-rolled SVG chart over adding a charting library.
